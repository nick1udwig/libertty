Self-hosted and private

The server runs on your own Linux or macOS machine, and your phone reaches it over Tailscale. Voxtype Mobile transcribes speech beside the server, so audio stays on your hardware.

Get started

Set up in three steps

  1. Install on your computer

    Run the one-line installer. It sets up the background service and offers Herdr, Voxtype Mobile and Tailscale.

  2. Join your tailnet

    Install Tailscale on your phone and sign in to the same tailnet. Setup configures private HTTPS access for libertty.

  3. Pair your phone

    Open the URL that setup prints, enter the pairing token, and add libertty to your home screen.
