Paper and Ink stay readable on grayscale and amber-lit screens. Open terminals reload to apply a new theme.

Swipe sideways to scroll, up to reveal terminal tabs, or down to hide them. Tap the selected space again, or hold any space, to open its tree of worktrees and panes. Hold, then drag beside a space to reorder, or onto it to stack. + creates a space in the current directory.

Status lanes group tabs into needs you, done, working and idle. Tap a lane's count to fold or unfold it. Dragging is paused while lanes are on, and your manual order returns when you switch back.

On mobile, the app resizes the shared terminal to fit your screen, including the desktop view. Desktop web views can reflow or pan at full width.
